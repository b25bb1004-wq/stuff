## What soak.rs does (exact)
- Uses `assay::Rng` only, seeds from `STRATA_SOAK_SEED` (default `0x5EED_798`), and runs for
  `STRATA_SOAK_MS` milliseconds per target (default 300, so the normal gate stays fast). Prints the
  seed and case count per target.
- Targets: mount and fsck of random and mutated disk images (MemDisk from mkfs plus random ops), and random Fs op sequences checked against fsck after each crash point.
- Inputs: half purely random bytes or text, half mutations (bit flips, truncation, splices,
  repeated chunks) of valid inputs the crate itself produces. Every call must return without
  panicking, within 1 second per case, and allocate at most 64 MiB (check sizes you pass).
- Where a round trip exists (encode then decode, print then parse), assert it on the valid inputs.

## Report
`reports/T79h.md` (at most 15 lines): targets, cases run at the default budget and with
`STRATA_SOAK_MS=60000`, and every defect found with its fix (file:line) or `none found`.
