## Tool
- `mercury`: `parse FILE` (raw request or response; prints fields), `serve DIR [--listen 127.0.0.1:8080]`
  (static files via `Router::static_dir`, Ctrl+C stops), `get http://HOST[:PORT]/PATH` (plain HTTP only; https
  gives exit 1 `mercury: https not supported`). Test `serve` on a free port in the test with a thread and
  `TcpStream`, then `get` against it.

## Tests
`tests/cli.rs` as `docs/APPS.md` says: `--help`, `--version`, every subcommand on a good input, one bad input each
(exit 1 and a `mercury:` message), an unknown subcommand (exit 2).

## Report
`reports/T86e.md` (at most 10 lines): subcommands, anything left out and why, tests, gate last line.
