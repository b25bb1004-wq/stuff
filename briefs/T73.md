## Design (fixed by the leader)
- `src/lang.rs` (all targets), host-tested, built on the AST (`logos::parser::parse`); no type inference needed:
  - `pub fn definition(src: &str, offset: usize) -> Option<(usize, usize)>`: the byte range of the declaration of the
    identifier under `offset`: a function, a type, a variant, a parameter, a `let`/`var` binding or a pattern binding,
    respecting block scope and shadowing (the innermost binding visible at that point wins). `None` when the cursor is
    not on an identifier, the name is a builtin/extern, or the source does not parse.
  - `pub fn hover(src: &str, offset: usize) -> Option<String>`: for a function, its signature printed as source
    (`fn max2(a: int, b: int) -> int`); for a type, its header; for a parameter or binding, `param a: int` /
    `let x: int` when annotated, `let x` when not; for a literal, its kind (`int literal`, `str literal`).
  - `pub fn format(src: &str) -> Result<String, String>`: `print(parse(src))`. If the source contains a comment,
    return `Err("format would drop comments")` and change nothing (the printer has no comments). Must be idempotent:
    `format(format(x)) == format(x)`.
- `src/wasm.rs` adds `athena_definition(ptr, len, offset) -> i32` (writes `start end` to the athena last buffer and
  returns its length, or 0 for none), `athena_hover(ptr, len, offset) -> len` (0 for none), `athena_format(ptr, len)
  -> len` (`ok\n<text>` or `error: <message>`).
- Editor (`athena.js`, `style.css`), tokens from `docs/FRONT.md` only:
  - Hover: resting the pointer 400 ms over a word (map pointer to offset through the highlighted pre) or pressing
    Ctrl/Cmd+I at the caret shows a small tooltip (`role="tooltip"`, `--panel`, `--line` border) near it; Esc or moving
    away hides it.
  - Go to definition: F12 or Ctrl/Cmd+click moves the caret to the declaration and selects its name; nothing happens
    (and a short status message says so) when there is none.
  - Format: a `Format` button in the top bar and Shift+Alt+F; on `Err` the status line shows the message and the text
    is untouched; on success the caret stays on the same line where possible, and the change is undoable with
    Ctrl/Cmd+Z (use `document.execCommand("insertText")` on the selected whole text, or keep your own undo snapshot).
  - Errors: as T72, plus the first diagnostic's message appears in the status line.
