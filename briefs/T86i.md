## Tool
- `helios`: `run GAME.logos [--ticks N] [--seed S]` runs the game headless and prints the world hash every 60 ticks and at the end, `record GAME.logos INPUTS -o REPLAY` and `replay GAME.logos REPLAY` (prints the final hash; same as the recording). Use the scripting and replay APIs the crate has; leave out what it lacks and say so.

each (exit 1 and a `helios:` message), an unknown subcommand (exit 2). Run `cargo fmt -p helios` before the gate.
